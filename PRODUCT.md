# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Cape Town small business owners who need a first website or a better one and do not code. They are busy running the business, often take orders by WhatsApp or social media, and want a clear price and a quick turnaround.

## Product Purpose
The Digital Pantry Web Studio's own site. It shows the portfolio and the packages, and turns visitors into enquiries through the contact form. Success means qualified enquiries that become signed projects.

## Positioning
Every site is built from scratch by one developer, Aaniquah, a Cape Town software engineer. No templates. Most projects are done within 5 to 7 business days.

## Operating Context
- Pages: Home, Portfolio, Pricing, Contact, Privacy.
- Visitors arrive from outreach emails, word of mouth and search, and compare packages before getting in touch.
- React site hosted on Netlify. Live at https://thedigitalpantry.netlify.app.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Packages (once-off, ZAR): Starter R600, Standard R1 000, Premium R1 600, Pro R2 500. Retainer R120/month for small edits. Prices are read from `artifacts/digital-pantry/src/pages/Pricing.tsx`; that file is the source of truth and prices are never hard-coded elsewhere from memory.
- Pro includes a lead capture form with instant alerts and leads saved to the client's own Google Sheet.
- Contact form collects personal details, so POPIA applies. The Privacy page must stay accurate.

## Brand Commitments
- Full Digital Pantry parent brand. HQ `brand/BRAND.md` is binding and overrides any design skill.
- Tagline: "Coded in Pink. Built to Perform."
- Voice: fun, warm, a little cheeky, human, never corporate. Pantry and kitchen wordplay ("Let's Get Cooking", "Fresh Out The Kitchen").
- The founder story and her pixel-art avatar are the face of the brand.

## Evidence on Hand
- Two live client sites in the portfolio: Stuff'd Bagels (https://stuffdbagels.netlify.app) and Beginners Madrasah (https://beginnersmadrasah.co.za).
- No client testimonials about the studio on file. Do not invent testimonials, client counts, reviews or results.

## Product Principles
1. Show the work and let the quality speak. Confident, not salesy.
2. Clear prices, no hidden fees.
3. Make it easy to get in touch: the enquiry is the goal of every page.
4. Never invent facts, reviews or figures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
HQ standards apply (`knowledge/tech/STANDARDS.md`): accessibility and Core Web Vitals are non-negotiable. Mobile-first.
